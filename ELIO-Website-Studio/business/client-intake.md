## Look & feel → `theme`
- Brand colors (or "use our logo colors"):
- Logo file (send PNG/SVG):
- 2–3 websites you like:

## Homepage message → `hero`
- What should a visitor do first? (call, book, get a quote, visit)
- Biggest reason customers choose you:
- A good photo for the top of the page (send it):

## Services → `services`
For each service: name, a 1–2 sentence description, and a price (optional).

## About → `about`
- How long in business? Who runs it? What area do you serve?
- What makes you different?

## Reviews → `testimonials`
- 2–4 **real** customer reviews, with the customer's permission (Google reviews work well).

## Extras
- Do you own a domain? Which one, and who is the registrar?
- Do you want a contact form? Which email should it send to?
- Google Business Profile set up? (Y/N)
