# Pricing & Sales Playbook

## Packages (starting points — adjust to your market)
| Package | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $499 one-time | One-page site, mobile-friendly, click-to-call, map |
| Business | $1,200 one-time | Up to 5 pages, contact form, basic SEO, Google Business setup |
| Care Plan | $49/month | Hosting, SSL, monthly edits, backups |

The Care Plan is where the steady income comes from: 20 clients × $49 = **$980/month** recurring.

## Who to sell to
Local businesses with **no website**, an outdated one, or only a Facebook page:
plumbers, roofers, landscapers, cleaners, salons, barbers, restaurants, food trucks, auto shops and contractors.

**How to find them:** search Google Maps for "[trade] near [city]" and note listings with no website link or a broken one.

## The "demo first" pitch (works well)
1. Pick a business and build a demo with `./new-client.sh their-name` (about 15 minutes, using details from their Google listing).
2. Host the demo somewhere free (see the README).
3. Reach out by phone, in person or by email:

> Hi {{Name}}, I noticed {{Business}} doesn't have a website yet, so I made you a free preview of what one could look like: {{link}}. If you like it, I can have it live on your own domain this week for ${{price}}. No pressure. Happy to change anything.

4. Follow up after 3 days and again after 7.

## Getting paid
- Use invoices from Stripe, Square or PayPal.
- 50% deposit before work, 50% at launch.
- Bill the Care Plan as a monthly subscription.

## Before you launch any client site
- [ ] Client approved all text and photos
- [ ] Phone and email links tested on a phone
- [ ] Reviews are real and used with permission
- [ ] Photos are the client's own, or properly licensed
- [ ] Domain is in the **client's** name (you manage it, they own it)
- [ ] Simple written agreement signed (scope, price, revisions, who owns what)

> Taxes, business registration and contracts: check your local requirements, or ask an accountant or lawyer.
