      Connect Netlify (free)

      Netlify hosts your demo websites online so customers can open them. You do this once.

      1. Go to app.netlify.com/signup and make a free account.
      2. Click your profile picture, then User settings → Applications.
      3. Under Personal access tokens, click New access token, name it "ELIO Studio", and copy it.
      4. Paste it below and click Connect.

      Connected. You can put demos online now.
